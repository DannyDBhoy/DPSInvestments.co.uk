# Devine Property Solutions

A responsive, five-page static website for a Fife property refurbishment business planning to begin in 2027. Charcoal and gold, with locally hosted assets and no build dependencies.

## Develop

From `/workspace/DPSInvestments.co.uk`:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 0.0.0.0
```

Pages: `index.html`, `about.html`, `plans.html`, `projects.html`, `approach.html`, and `sell-your-property.html`. Styles and navigation are in `assets/`.

## Deployment

Serve the repository root on any static hosting service. This draft has not been published or connected to the existing domain.

## Content status

Company name, Fife focus, planned 2027 start, and cash-led business model are based on the owner's brief. No projects, track record, company registration details, or investment offerings have been invented. The owner supplied the public enquiry email and phone number. The enquiry button opens the visitor’s email app; no form backend or data collection service is configured. Email delivery and mailbox availability have not been verified. The architectural illustration is decorative, not a photograph of a company project.
